# Nova Strike

A fast arcade space shooter that runs in any browser. No install, no dependencies: open `index.html` and play.

**Play now:** https://aeldtagaming-ship-it.github.io/nova-strike/
**itch.io:** https://aeldtagaming-ship-it.itch.io/nova-strike

## Controls
- Move: WASD or arrow keys (drag on touch screens, auto-fire)
- Fire: hold Space
- Bomb: B (clears all enemy bullets and damages everything); tap the BOMB button on touch
- Focus mode: hold Shift (slower movement, shows your tiny hitbox)
- Pause: P or Esc. Mute: M

## Features
- Endless waves with a gentle start and a steep ramp
- Five enemy types: drones, zig-zaggers, armored tanks, kamikaze divers, snipers with laser sights
- Three boss fights (Dreadnought, Specter, Hydra) every 5 waves, with a rage phase below 50% health
- Pick 1 of 3 upgrades after every wave: rapid fire, heavy rounds, piercing, homing missiles, wingman drones, magnet, shield regen, hull plating, bombs, weapon level
- Combo multiplier, perfect-wave bonus, power-ups (G weapon, S shield, + health, B bomb)
- Global online leaderboard (world top 10, powered by Supabase) plus local top-5 scores
- Glow effects, nebula parallax background, particles, screen shake, synthesized sound
- `?demo` in the URL runs an autopilot, handy for recording footage

Built with AI assistance. Single HTML file, plain canvas and JavaScript.
