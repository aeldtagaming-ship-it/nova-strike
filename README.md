# Nova Strike

A fast arcade space shooter that runs in any browser. No install, no dependencies: open `index.html` and play.

**Play now:** https://aeldtagaming-ship-it.github.io/nova-strike/
**itch.io:** https://aeldtagaming-ship-it.itch.io/nova-strike

## Controls
- Move: WASD or arrow keys (drag on touch screens, auto-fire)
- Fire: hold Space
- Bomb: B (clears enemy bullets and damages everything); BOMB button on touch (bottom right)
- Ship special: E or V; E button on touch (bottom left)
- Focus mode: hold Shift (slower movement, shows your tiny hitbox)
- Pause: P or Esc. Mute: M
- Menu: Left/Right to pick a ship, D for the daily challenge, S for the hangar, H to replay the tutorial

## Features
- Endless waves with a gentle start and a steep ramp, plus an interactive first-run tutorial
- Six enemy types: drones, zig-zaggers, armored tanks, kamikaze divers, snipers, splitters. Gold elites from wave 8
- Four bosses (Dreadnought, Specter, Hydra, Overmind) every 5 waves, with a rage phase below 50% health
- Hazards: meteor showers and laser grids
- Graze scoring for close bullet passes, with a free bomb every 25 grazes
- Three ships (Viper, Falcon, Titan), each with a special: Phase Shift, Overdrive, Shockwave
- Hangar: earn credits each run and buy 8 skins, 4 guns (Pulse, Scatter, Railgun, Seeker) and 6 ship mods
- Pick 1 of 3 upgrades after every wave: rapid fire, heavy rounds, piercing, homing missiles, wingmen, lucky hits, magnet, shield regen, hull plating, bombs, weapon level
- Combo multiplier, perfect-wave bonus, power-ups (G weapon, S shield, + health, B bomb, R overdrive)
- Daily challenge: the same seeded run for everyone each UTC day, with its own leaderboard
- Global online leaderboard (world top 10, powered by Supabase) plus local top-5 scores
- Glow effects, planets and sector tints, particles, hit-stop, screen shake, synthesized sound
- `?demo` in the URL runs an autopilot, handy for recording footage

Built with AI assistance. Single HTML file, plain canvas and JavaScript.
